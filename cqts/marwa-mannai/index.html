Research

From the mathematics of topological order to working quantum devices

Quantum materials, information, sensing, computing and algorithms — and the long-term necessity of topological stabilization through topologically ordered phases of quantum materials.

Quantum materials Quantum information Quantum sensing Quantum computing & algorithms Topological order
Theory

Topological phases, branes and quantum programming

Understanding strongly coupled/correlated condensed matter, such as fractional quantum Hall systems, via geometric engineering on branes — alongside topology-aware quantum programming languages based on stable homotopy theory.

Topological order on the nLab
Quantum Labs

Cold atoms for sensing and secure communication

Spin-based cold-atom platforms for quantum sensing and related technologies, such as quantum key distribution, that are near-term application ready.
